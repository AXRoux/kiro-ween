# Product: Trick or Read

A talking scrying mirror for blind and low-vision trick-or-treaters. Hold a candy up to the webcam; the mirror names
it out loud, warns about the allergens you said matter to you, and says whether the wrapper is still sealed.

## Who it is for
- **Primary:** a blind or low-vision person sorting their own Halloween haul, without asking anyone to read wrappers.
- **Secondary:** the parent, sibling or friend beside them, who sees the haunted interface and can confirm a warning.

## What matters, in order
1. **Never say something is safe when we do not know.** Allergen facts from a label (Open Food Facts) are stated as
   facts; anything the vision model infers is said as "likely" and ends with "ask someone to check the label".
2. **Hands-free.** Hold the candy still and it scans itself. Every action also has a key and a voice command.
3. **Speech first, sight second.** Everything the screen shows is spoken or announced to a screen reader.
4. **The costume serves the function.** Every spooky element carries information: the mirror frames where to hold
   the candy, the runes show the step, the candles and the spirit board show the verdict.

## Verdicts
- **Beware:** an allergen on the user's list is listed or likely, or the wrapper is opened, torn or unwrapped.
- **Ask someone:** we could not read an allergen label, or the read was uncertain.
- **Safe:** sealed, and the label lists none of the user's allergens.
